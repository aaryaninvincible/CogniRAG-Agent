GLOBAL FINTECH COMPLIANCE AND RISK GOVERNANCE HANDBOOK (2026 EDITION)

SECTION 1: ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) AND MANDATORY THRESHOLDS
All financial transactions crossing domestic or cross-border payment rails are subject to real-time screening.
- Mandatory Currency Transaction Reporting (CTR) triggers automatically on all cash-equivalent transactions equal to or exceeding $10,000 USD within a rolling 24-hour window.
- Suspicious Activity Reports (SAR) must be filed with FinCEN within strictly 30 calendar days following initial detection of suspicious behavior.
- High-risk jurisdiction wire transfers require secondary compliance officer sign-off for any transfer exceeding $5,000 USD.

SECTION 2: CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE AND KYC TIERS
- Tier 1 (Basic Verification): Identity verification via government ID and mobile SMS OTP. Daily transaction limit is $2,500 USD.
- Tier 2 (Enhanced Due Diligence): Proof of address, biometric face match, and tax identification. Daily transaction limit is $50,000 USD.
- Tier 3 (Institutional): Full beneficial ownership audit, corporate registry, and LEI validation. Unlimited volume with dedicated account manager review.

SECTION 3: DISPUTE AND CHARGEBACK BENCHMARKS
The platform enforces a maximum allowable Chargeback-to-Transaction ratio of 0.90% (90 basis points). Merchants exceeding this threshold for two consecutive calendar months are enrolled into the Excessive Chargeback Program (ECP) with a mandatory $25 penalty fee per dispute.
